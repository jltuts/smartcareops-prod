# Fictional Uncertainty and Conflict Policy

Policy ID: `SCO-DEMO-UNCERTAINTY-03`

Version: `m60-fictional-v1`

Scope: SmartCareOps fictional demonstration and evaluation only.

## 1. Missing information

State material missing information explicitly. Do not replace unavailable
evidence with general knowledge, assumptions, or information from another run.

## 2. Conflicting evidence

When approved sources conflict, cite every conflicting passage, describe the
conflict, and retain human review. The agent must not choose one source and hide
the other or manufacture a resolution.

## 3. Retrieval failure

Empty, unavailable, stale, or invalid policy retrieval is a visible evidence
limitation. It must not weaken the evidence rules or permit an unsupported
clinical statement.
