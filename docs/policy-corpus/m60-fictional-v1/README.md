# SmartCareOps fictional policy corpus

This corpus is authored for the SmartCareOps AI Builder Cup demonstration. It
defines evidence-handling and AI safety controls. It contains no treatment
guideline and is not approved for clinical use.

Permitted use: SmartCareOps fictional-data development, testing, evaluation,
and demonstration.

Prohibited content: Patient records, transcripts, uploaded laboratory reports,
model output, operational state, credentials, and secrets.

The SmartCareOps product owner approved this fictional demonstration corpus on
2026-10-16. The approval applies only to fictional-data development, testing,
evaluation, and demonstration. It is not approval for clinical use.
